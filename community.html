Community Guidelines

Peace begins with how we treat each other.

Imagine Peace … · Pass the Peace is a space for sharing perspectives, experiences and hopes across generations and borders.

Everyone is welcome to contribute. We simply ask you to:

  • Be respectful. Different experiences and opinions are welcome. Hate speech, discrimination, threats and personal attacks are not.
  • Speak from your own perspective. Share your thoughts honestly and listen to others with an open mind.
  • Protect privacy. Please don't share personal information about yourself or others.
  • Keep it constructive. This is a space for dialogue, not advertising, spam or party-political campaigning. Political perspectives and constructive criticism are welcome.
  • Respect different experiences. Peace means different things to different people, especially those affected by war and violence.

Reporting and moderation

We may remove contributions that violate these guidelines. If you believe a contribution crosses a line, you can use the report function on the Peace Wall.

Thank you for helping us create a space where every voice can be heard. 💜
