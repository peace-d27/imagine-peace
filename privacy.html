Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

At a glance. Contributions are published immediately on the public Peace Wall. You can publish anonymously or with your first name, last-name initial and city. Please do not submit sensitive information or information about other people.

2. What this website does

Imagine Peace is a non-commercial pilot website. You can read the website without creating an account. If you submit a response to one of the three Peace Cards, we process the card number, your response, the date and time of submission, your publication choice, and, if you choose named attribution, your first name, last-name initial and city. Anonymous contributions do not include these attribution fields in the contribution record.

The website does not ask for an email address when you contribute. Please do not include personal information about others or special-category personal data in your response.

3. Publication and legal basis

By actively agreeing to publication and submitting a contribution, you consent to the public display of your response and chosen attribution on the Peace Wall (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Publication takes place immediately, without prior editorial review. Anyone on the internet may read, copy or share publicly displayed contributions. We cannot reliably remove copies made by third parties.

4. Withdrawal and removal

You can withdraw your consent for future publication at any time by emailing christiane.lemberg@lemberg.eu. Please identify the contribution by quoting its text and, if possible, its approximate submission date. We will review and process your request without undue delay. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. You can also report a contribution using the link beneath it.

5. Reports and moderation

When you report a contribution, the website processes its identifier, your stated reason and the time of the report. Reports are used to review potentially inappropriate or unlawful content and to protect the discussion space. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating and moderating the Peace Wall responsibly (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Do not include personal information in report reasons unless necessary.

6. Hosting and technical data

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub. The Peace Wall and reporting function use Supabase as a backend service. To deliver the website, process submissions, prevent abuse and maintain security, these providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request metadata and service logs. Such technical data may be processed even if you publish anonymously. Processing necessary to deliver and secure the website is based on our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), subject to the applicable balancing assessment.

Service providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. The applicable contractual arrangements, international-transfer safeguards, infrastructure locations and provider-specific log retention depend on the services and account configuration in use. Please contact us for information about the arrangements applicable to this pilot.

7. Retention

Public contributions are kept while needed for the pilot and may be removed earlier following a valid request, moderation decision or closure of the project. We review their continued necessity at least annually. Moderation reports are retained only as long as needed to handle the relevant issue and related legal obligations. Technical logs are subject to the applicable service-provider retention settings and policies.

8. Participation by minors

During the pilot, the submission form is intended for people aged 16 or over. If you are under 18, please do not include identifying details. Younger visitors may explore the cards without submitting personal information. The age confirmation is self-declared and does not constitute an identity check.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable legal conditions, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority. For requests, contact christiane.lemberg@lemberg.eu.

10. Changes

We may update this policy if the pilot or its technical or legal arrangements change. The current version will be available on this page.
