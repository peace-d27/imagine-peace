Why Imagine Peace?

Peace is not a certainty.

For someone who has experienced war, peace may mean safety. For someone who remembers the Cold War, freedom. For someone facing violence at home, a life without fear. For others, peace means dignity, justice, belonging or simply being able to live and let live.

Different experiences. Different meanings of peace. Yet all are connected — through our rights, our relationships, our communities and the world we share.

Imagine Peace invites you to explore these perspectives. Not to find one perfect definition, but to discover what we can learn from one another.
