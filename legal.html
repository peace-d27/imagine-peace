About the project

Imagine Peace is an independent, non-commercial pilot initiative operated by Christiane Lemberg in her personal capacity. It explores ideas developed in the context of peace advocacy within Zonta District 27, but it is not an official website of Zonta International or Zonta District 27. Its publication does not imply institutional endorsement.

The Peace Wall contains contributions submitted by visitors. These contributions express the views of their respective authors, not necessarily those of the website operator or Zonta.

Contact and reports

For questions about the website, requests to remove a contribution, or notices of potentially unlawful content, please contact christiane.lemberg@lemberg.eu. You may also use the reporting link displayed beneath each Peace Wall contribution.

Editorial information

This website is intended to encourage dialogue about peace and does not provide legal advice.
